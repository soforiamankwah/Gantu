Modernising BSS/OSS without a big-bang rewrite

Telecom platforms can be modernised step by step. Here is how to reduce risk while you do it.

Telecoms | GANTU Insights

Operators carry decades of billing, CRM and service systems. Replacing everything at once is risky and slow.

Map before you move

An assessment of systems, interfaces and dependencies shows where complexity and cost are concentrated, so you modernise in the right order.

Use APIs as the bridge

Application integration and APIs let new digital services sit on top of existing platforms, giving customers better journeys before the back end is fully replaced.

Prove with a pilot

Move one product line or customer segment first. Use the lessons to refine the plan before wider rollout.

The bottom line

Modernisation succeeds when it is paced by business value and supported by a team that can operate the platform after go-live.
