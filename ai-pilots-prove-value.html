Why AI pilots stall, and how to prove value in 90 days

AI & Data | 2026-10-06 | GANTU Insights

Many organisations run an AI pilot, admire the demo and then watch it fade. The technology is rarely the problem. The pilot was too broad, the data was not ready, or nobody owned the business result.

Start with one decision

Pick a single process where better prediction or automation changes a number leaders already track: handling time, fraud losses, forecast accuracy. A narrow question gives a clear answer.

Fix the data path first

Document intelligence, analytics and AI assistants all depend on reliable data. Spend the first weeks mapping sources, access and quality, so the pilot runs on data you can trust.

Plan for production on day one

Agree who runs the model, how it is monitored and what happens when it is wrong. A pilot that cannot be operated is a demo, not a proof of value.

The bottom line

A 90-day pilot with a defined owner, a measurable target and an operating plan gives executives what they need to decide: scale, adjust or stop.
