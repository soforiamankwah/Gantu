Digital wallets and payment orchestration: what operators must get right

Commerce & Payments | 2026-10-13 | GANTU Insights

A digital wallet looks simple to the customer. Behind it sit payment routes, billing, subscriptions, fraud checks and daily reconciliation. Operators who treat these as one system launch faster and lose less revenue.

Orchestrate, do not hard-wire

Payment orchestration lets you route transactions across providers, add new methods and respond to outages without rebuilding the platform each time.

Make reconciliation a feature

Finance teams need every transaction matched and explained. Automated reconciliation and revenue management reduce leakage and shorten month-end.

Build compliance in

KYC, fraud analytics and reporting belong in the design from the start, especially for licensed operators and fintechs.

The bottom line

Get the foundations right and wallets, subscriptions and marketplaces become repeatable revenue streams rather than one-off projects.
